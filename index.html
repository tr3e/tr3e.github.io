先为力胜

I am a research scientist at ByteDance Seed. My work sits at the intersection of graphics, vision and robotics, with a focus on video generation and embodied AI.

I received my Ph.D. in computer science from ShanghaiTech University in 2025, advised by Prof. Lan Xu and Prof. Jingyi Yu. My ultimate aspiration is to realise human-centered super agents that free people from tedious and heavy work.
