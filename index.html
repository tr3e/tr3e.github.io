ShanghaiTech University - 上海科技大学 - SIST - VDI


I am a research scientist at ByteDance Seed in Shanghai, China.
Prior to that, I received my Ph.D. in computer science at VRVC Lab, ShanghaiTech University, advised by Prof. Lan Xu and Prof. Jingyi Yu.
I got my B.E. in computer software engineering from UESTC.

My research focuses on the intersection of graphics, vision and robotics, especially video generation and digital humans.
My ultimate aspiration is to realize human-centered embodied intelligent agents that liberate us human beings from tedious and heavy work.

I'm open to any opportunities for cooperation and discussion.
If you are interested, please don't hesitate to contact me.

My current research interests include:

  • Video generation
  • Digital humans
  • Embodied AI
