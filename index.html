Research Scientist · Shanghai

Building intelligent systems that understand our world.

I am a research scientist at ByteDance Seed. My research sits at the intersection of graphics, vision, and robotics, with a focus on video generation and embodied AI.

I received my Ph.D. from ShanghaiTech University, advised by Prof. Lan Xu and Prof. Jingyi Yu. My goal is to build human-centered agents that free people from tedious and physically demanding work.
